Scriptorium - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Scriptorium ("the app") is developed by muhsintags. This page explains what data is collected when you use the app and how it is used.

Data We Collect

  • Account Information: If you choose to sign in, your name, email address, and profile information are retrieved via your Google Account through Firebase Authentication.
  • Usage Data: Your in-app preferences (such as reading history and favorite texts) may be stored on Firebase Firestore.
  • Local Storage: Texts you download are stored locally on your device (via a Room database) for offline access.

How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used solely to provide app functionality: managing your account, syncing your preferences across devices, and personalizing your app experience. Your data is not sold for advertising purposes or shared with third parties.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore) - Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Translate API (for text translation)

Data Security

Your data is protected using industry-standard security measures. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Deleting Your Data

If you wish to delete your account and associated data, you can request deletion from the in-app account settings, or contact us at the email/link below.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
