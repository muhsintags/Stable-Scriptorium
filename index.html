What it does

A library, not a feed

No daily-verse popups, no accounts, no ads competing with the text. Scriptorium opens to a shelf of books and gets out of the way.

Fully offline

Each text downloads once and is stored locally on your device. After that the app never needs a connection again.

Comparative reading

Open two texts next to each other in three different viewing layouts and follow the same idea across traditions.

Built-in translation

Integrated translation support, so a passage in an unfamiliar language is one tap away from being readable.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no background calls. Nothing about your reading leaves the phone.

Modern, quiet UI

Written entirely in Jetpack Compose with Material 3 — dark, legible typography built for long reading sessions.

Yours to fork

MIT licensed. Translate the interface, add a text, or turn it into a philosophy library of Stoic and classical works.

The centrepiece

Read two traditions at once

Comparative mode is what makes Scriptorium different from every single-tradition reader: two texts, one screen, three layouts.
